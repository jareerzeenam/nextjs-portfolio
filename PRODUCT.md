## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads and technical recruiters (UK first, open to relocation) evaluating Jareer Zeenam for a full-stack / PHP-Laravel software engineer role. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a job application, usually skim in under a minute, often on a phone, and decide whether to shortlist or contact him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio whose job is to get Jareer an interview. Success: a hiring manager understands within the first screen who he is, what he builds and that he is available now, finds proof (shipped work, scale, recommendations), and contacts him or downloads the resume.

## Positioning

A full-stack engineer with 6+ years across healthcare, education, e-commerce and agency work, specialist in PHP/Laravel with Node.js, GraphQL, TypeScript, Next.js and Vue, who has scaled a platform serving tens of thousands of concurrent users, and who also holds an MSc in AI and Data Science (Distinction). The site itself is proof of front-end and 3D craft.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare several candidates quickly. The resume PDF (public/Jareer_Zeenam_Resume.pdf) is the canonical document; the site must agree with it. Contact is by email (and LinkedIn). Status: open to work, available immediately, open to relocation, full UK driving licence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js (App Router, v16) + React 19 + Tailwind 3 + framer-motion; three.js / @react-three/fiber / drei already installed.
- Routes: `/` (portfolio), `/resume` (PDF viewer + download), `/generate-qr-code`, sitemap.
- 3D budget (user decision): one signature real-time 3D scene plus at most one more 3D moment, with still fallbacks for low-end devices and reduced motion. The rest is fast HTML.
- Must stay readable, indexable and fast on phones.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim traces to the resume, a project or a named recommendation.
2. Availability is obvious: open to work and how to reach him are never more than one screen away.
3. The craft is the pitch: the 3D and motion show front-end skill, but never block reading the facts.
4. Fast on a phone: the hiring manager on mobile gets the whole story without waiting on WebGL.
