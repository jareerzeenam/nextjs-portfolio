# Home page (`/`)

Scope: the single-page portfolio. Mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers and technical recruiters (PRODUCT.md). Action: email Jareer or open the resume.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Jareer's career is a console overworld. Each role is a floating diorama island on one dotted path from 2018 to now, and choosing a level flies the camera there. This replaces the category default of a name on the left, a spinning 3D blob on the right and cards below.

OWN-WORLD: A dusk sky ground (#141C2B) with four flat inks: bone #ECE3CF, moss #6E9A73, ochre #E3A93B for the path, selection and primary action, and clay #C8553D used sparingly. Toon-shaded low-poly islands with no gloss or bloom haze. Archivo is the only family: expanded caps for stage titles, normal width for body, condensed caps for HUD labels. Stage plates are square-cornered with a 1px bone hairline and an ochre notch on the selected one.

STORY: In the first screen the visitor learns that he is a full-stack engineer, open to work and in London, and sees the whole career laid out as a world. Scrolling proves it level by level with literal names and real numbers, then recommendations. The visitor ends at "Open to work", with an email button and the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed WebGL overworld with seven islands along a glowing dotted path. A stage plate at the lower left holds the h1 "Jareer Zeenam, full-stack software engineer", the line "Laravel · Node.js · GraphQL · Next.js. 6+ years. Open to work, London.", and the actions Email me (primary, ochre) and Resume. The level selector at the bottom (HTML, keyboard-operable) is mirrored in 3D.

FORM: Level-select overworld. Candidate 4 on the ordered list. seed:04f1322ff7bb402c. Raises: WPA four flat inks; multiplane text on the nearest plane; quote-grammar literal level names; VU spring ballistics; annual one family and a strict grid.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

Hovering, tapping or arrowing through levels springs the camera to that island. The stage card swaps to the role, the years and one literal achievement.
